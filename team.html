Dr. Daniel Omoro Achach

Law and Policy Expert

Dr. Daniel Omoro Achach is a distinguished international trade law and policy expert, researcher, and legal practitioner with extensive experience across Africa, Europe, and multilateral institutions. Holding a Ph.D. in International Trade Law from Bond University and an MSc. from Lund University, Dr. Achach specializes in trade remedies, regional integration, digital trade, trade in services, and dispute resolution. He has spearheaded high-level consulting assignments for major continental and international bodies—including the AfCFTA Secretariat, EAC Secretariat, COMESA Secretariat & Court of Justice, UNECA, and GOPA Worldwide Consultants—guiding governments and private sectors on policy harmonization, regulatory audits, and trade negotiations. Alongside his advisory work, he serves as a Trainer and Facilitator in International Trade Policy at the Eastern and Southern Africa Management Institute (ESAMI) / trapca in conjunction with Lund University. Dr. Achach also serves as a Board Member/Director at Lake Victoria North Water Works Development Agency.
