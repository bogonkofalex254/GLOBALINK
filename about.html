About GlobaLink Mentors

Built by mentors, for mentees who needed one first.

We're a Kenyan-founded, community-driven organisation that places trained volunteer mentors inside schools for structured, year-long relationships — not one-off talks.

How it started

A classroom visit that didn't end

In 2013, a small group of university graduates returned to their old secondary school to give a single career talk. Students kept asking to meet again. That "one more session" became weekly visits, then a timetable, then a curriculum — and eventually GlobaLink Mentors.

What started with 14 students in one Nairobi classroom now runs inside more than 60 schools across four counties, with a full-time team supporting hundreds of volunteer mentors.

OUR VALUES

01

Consistency

A mentor who shows up once won't change much. Ours commit to a full academic year, minimum.

02

Dignity

Every mentee is treated as capable first, and in-need second. We build on strengths.

03

Evidence

We track attendance, grades and wellbeing so we know what's actually working.

04

Partnership

We don't replace schools or parents — we work inside their existing structures.

MISSION AND VISION

Mission: to give every student in a partner school access to a trained, consistent mentor who helps them stay in school, build life skills, and plan a realistic path forward.

Vision: a generation of young East Africans who had someone believe in them early enough to believe in themselves.
