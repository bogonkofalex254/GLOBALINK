What we deliver

Structured programmes, not one-off visits.

Every service below is delivered inside a partner school, tracked over a full academic year, and reviewed each term with the school's guidance department.

01

One-on-One Mentorship

A mentee is matched with the same trained mentor for a minimum of one academic year. Weekly 45-minute sessions cover schoolwork, confidence-building and whatever is actually on the student's mind that week.

02

Career & College Guidance

Subject-choice clinics, university and TVET application support, scholarship search assistance, and workplace exposure trips for students in their final two years of secondary school.

03

Life Skills Workshops

Group sessions on financial literacy, communication, healthy relationships, digital safety and self-advocacy, run termly across every partner school.

04

School Partnership & Teacher Training

We embed inside a school's existing guidance department, training teachers in basic mentorship skills and co-designing a referral process for students who need extra support.

05

Wellbeing & Counselling Referrals

Trained staff identify students who need professional counselling and connect families with vetted local services, at no cost to the household.

06

Corporate & Volunteer Mentor Programmes

We recruit, screen and train working professionals and university students as volunteer mentors, then match and support them for the full commitment period.

How a partnership starts

From first conversation to first session

1

Introductory Visit

We meet the head teacher and guidance department to understand the school's needs.

2

Student Selection

Teachers and counsellors help identify students who'd benefit most from a mentor.

3

Mentor Matching

We pair each student with a trained mentor based on interests and personality.

4

Ongoing Support

Weekly sessions run all year, with termly reviews shared with the school.
